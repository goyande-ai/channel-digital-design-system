# Channel Digital Logos

## channel-logo-mark.png

The Channel Digital mark, 840 x 596, transparent.

**Use on slides:**
- Top right of the cover strip: 34px wide on the 720px canvas
- Above the title on every content slide: 52px wide on the 720px canvas

**Properties:**
- Multi-colour and cannot be recoloured
- Place it on `white` or `grey-pale` only
- Never on `red-dark`

## channel-digital-logo.png

The full logo: the mark with the "channel DIGITAL" wordmark to its right, 1326 x 468, transparent.

**Use where the name must appear with the mark:**
- Width: do not show wider than about 660px on a 1920px slide
- The wordmark was enlarged from the live website; edges are slightly soft
- **Replace when the agency supplies a vector logo**

**Placement:**
- Place on `white` or `grey-pale`
- The wordmark is dark grey and disappears on `red-dark`
- Do not redraw, recolour, or crop
- Never set the wordmark in another typeface
